# Brand

Ollupi's name, mark, icon and wordmark, and where they appear. The interface's visual language is on [Design language](/desktop/design).

::: info Full page in Chinese
This page is being translated. The complete version is [in Chinese](/zh/desktop/brand).
:::

In short:

- **Name:** Ollupi, said oh-LOO-pee. The lupi in its name is Italian for wolves: awake at night, loyal to its own, keeping its ground. Ollupi works the same way: it keeps going when you walk away, it remembers you, and it asks before it touches anything of yours.
- **Definition:** a personal assistant that keeps working. **Line:** Hand it over. Walk away.
- **Mark:** a little wolf sitting in front of the moon, on an oat app icon lit from above, the same family as the interface's stone and moss.
- **Dusk theme:** the same wolf drawn in a dusk sky with a glowing moon, fine grain and edges that dissolve, with a little savanna around it (grass, a far acacia, fireflies, birds); it becomes the Dock icon and the round mark while the dusk theme is on.
- **Poses:** six poses of the same little wolf, used only in empty states and on first use.
- **Files:** all of the brand lives in `assets/brand/` in ollupi-desktop, generated from one drawing by `make.py`; the engine's README and the docs site take their marks from there.

# Design language

The desktop app's visual language is called **stone and moss**: a guide map to your own errands. Each workspace is an area you recognise by its colour at a glance; inside a conversation the result comes first, and the work behind it folds into one plain sentence. Every component uses only the semantic tokens in `packages/ui/src/theme.css`. The name and the mark are on [Brand](/desktop/brand).

The full page covers:

- Who it is for, and principle zero: AI makes things simpler
- Information hierarchy: what needs you, the result, the process, the meta

- The window: a stone ground, a paper sheet, the sidebar as a map of coloured areas
- Tokens: colours (light and dark), type, radii, shadows, motion
- Component notes: results first, the approval card, the new-task suggestions, panels that lead with a sentence
